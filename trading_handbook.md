# Nexus AI: The Ultimate Trader's Handbook

This manual is designed to teach you how to extract maximum value from the AI Trading Assistant while **strictly minimizing your risk of capital loss** when trading on live exchanges like Binance.

> [!CAUTION]
> **No AI is 100% accurate.** Markets are chaotic. This system provides a mathematical edge, but if you abandon risk management, you will lose money. Follow these rules religiously.

---

## 🛑 Rule 1: The Mandatory Incubation Period
Do not instantly deposit funds into Binance. 

When you first launch the app, the **Adaptive Memory Engine** has zero historical data. It needs time to learn the market's behavior and calibrate its confidence multiplier.
1. Go to the **Paper Trading** dashboard.
2. Manually enter the AI's signals as simulated trades.
3. Let the autonomous execution loop hit Stop Losses and Take Profits.
4. **Action:** Do this until the global *Lifetime Win Rate* on your dashboard stabilizes above 55%. Only then should you consider real capital.

---

## 📊 Rule 2: Respect the Market Regime
The most powerful feature of your dashboard is the **Macro Market Regime** widget. It tells you the overarching state of the market. You must obey its tactical advice.

- 🟢 **Bullish / Bearish Trend**: The easiest markets to trade. Follow the AI's directional signals aggressively.
- 🟣 **Ranging / Consolidation**: The market is moving sideways. Do not expect massive breakouts. Take profits early and trade "mean reversion" (buying the bottom of the range, selling the top).
- 🟠 **High Volatility Chop**: The market is erratic. **Reduce your normal position size by half.** The AI's Stop Losses will be wider here due to high ATR.

> [!WARNING]
> If the dashboard flashes the red **⚠️ EXTREME VOLATILITY DETECTED** banner, **STOP TRADING**. This indicates algorithmic liquidations and unpredictable spikes. Sit on your hands until the market calms down into an "Expansion" or "Normal" volatility state.

---

## 🎯 Rule 3: The Confidence Threshold
The Live Dashboard displays a large **Confidence Score (%)**. This score is dynamically adjusted by the Adaptive Memory system based on the AI's recent winning or losing streaks.

- **Confidence > 80%**: Prime setup. All technicals align (MTF trends, RSI momentum, NLP Sentiment). Standard position size.
- **Confidence 60% - 79%**: Mediocre setup. Conflicting data (e.g., trend is bullish but NLP news sentiment is "Fear"). Consider passing, or use half-size.
- **Confidence < 60%**: **DO NOT TRADE.** The market is too ambiguous.

---

## 🛡️ Rule 4: Emotionless Risk Management (ATR)
At the bottom of the AI Trade Plan card, you will see a grid containing the **Optimal Entry**, **Take Profit**, and **Stop Loss**.

The AI calculates the Stop Loss mathematically using **Average True Range (ATR)**. This means the stop is placed exactly outside the market's current natural noise.
> [!IMPORTANT]
> When you enter a trade on Binance, **immediately place your Stop Loss exactly where the AI tells you to.** 
> Never mentally say "I'll close it manually if it drops." You will freeze, and your account will be liquidated. Never widen the Stop Loss once the trade is open.

---

## 🔮 Rule 5: Pre-Empt the News with Scenarios
Crypto markets are highly susceptible to macroeconomic news (CPI data, Federal Reserve rate hikes, SEC announcements).

Before entering a trade during a news-heavy day:
1. Navigate to the **AI Scenarios** tab (`/scenarios`).
2. Ask the Quantitative Forecaster: *"What happens to this setup if the CPI data comes in higher than expected in 30 minutes?"*
3. The AI will cross-reference the live state and give you a probabilistic breakdown. If the bearish probability is high, close your trades *before* the news hits.

---

## 📓 Rule 6: Cure the "Tilt" (Revenge Trading)
The number one reason traders lose money on Binance is human emotion—specifically, taking a loss and immediately forcing a bad trade to make the money back.

1. After every session, open the **Trade Journal** (`/journal`).
2. Log your trades and honestly input your emotional state (e.g., *Anxious*, *Overconfident*, *Frustrated*).
3. The Behavioral AI will analyze your journal entries. If it detects that you consistently lose money during "Frustrated" states or during the Asian trading session, it will explicitly warn you. **Listen to it.**
